What's your experience with programming?

I've been programming for years. I started when I was 10 years old. I made simple websites and SmileBasic programs. This got me interested in programming.
I started modding SMG2 in 2020 and it taught me several things: C++, PowerPC ASM, and more.
Then I went to university and learned even more things about programming.
That taught me Python, Kotlin, and fundamental CS concepts. I expect to graduate in Fall 2026.
You can see some of the projects I've worked on over the years on the Projects page.

Why do you like Super Mario Galaxy 1 and 2?

They are the greatest games of all time in my opinion. The concept of new types of
gravity combined with a pleasant orchestrated soundtrack and fantastic
constantly changing levels makes the galaxy games so great.
